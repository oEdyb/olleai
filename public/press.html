Bio

Olle Dyberg has an MSc in Engineering Physics from Luleå University of Technology and makes content on AI as @olleai. He trapped single proteins with light as a biophysics researcher at the Adolphe Merkle Institute in Fribourg, Switzerland, and left a PhD position in 2026 to build with AI. His videos have 4.4M views on TikTok and 476K on YouTube (Nov 2026). He is building Klippt, a recording and editing app, and lives on Tjörn on the Swedish west coast.
